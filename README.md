## AI-first product engineering

I treat AI as a practical part of the product-development process—not a substitute for product judgment or accountability. Clear problem framing, requirements, architecture direction, priorities, and acceptance criteria help AI agents contribute useful implementation work. I then validate the result against the intended user experience and product outcome.

## My role and AI agents

**I lead:** product vision, discovery, requirements, prioritization, UX direction, architecture and technology choices, acceptance criteria, and quality decisions.

**AI agents support:** exploration, implementation, and other engineering tasks within that direction. They can produce substantial portions of the work; I orchestrate the workflow, review and test outcomes, and remain accountable for what is delivered.
